# 🚗 Car Garage Management System

A backend-driven **Car Garage Management System** designed to automate and streamline end-to-end garage operations. The platform reduces manual effort by managing vehicle service workflows, job cards, inventory, billing, and customer records in a structured and scalable way.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- Customer and vehicle profile management  
- Job card creation and service tracking  
- Inventory and spare parts management  
- Billing and invoice generation  
- Automated service workflow management  
- Centralized view of garage operations  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Backend:** PHP / Python  
- **Database:** MySQL  
- **APIs:** RESTful services  
- **Tools & Environment:** Git, Linux  

---

## 🧠 System Overview
- Backend-first architecture with clear separation of concerns  
- Modular design for easy feature expansion  
- Workflow automation to reduce manual operational tasks  
- Designed for reliability, maintainability, and clarity  

---
